How this room drafts
Starting assumption only — it is corrected against the picks as they come in, so by the middle rounds the app is using what your league actually did rather than what you told it.
Replacement baseline
Which player counts as freely available. A is the number derived from your lineup, B counts weekly starters only and runs RB-heavy, C counts everyone rostered and pushes TE and QB out of the early rounds.
Boom influence — 20%
0% = pure expert consensus. Higher lets 2024-25 spike-week rates move players within their position. The data is backward-looking, so it says nothing about rookies and can misjudge anyone whose situation changed.
Floor influence — 25%
How far week-to-week consistency and games played may move a player inside his position. One bottom score ends your season and there is no next week to make it back, so a volatile player and a player who misses a game are the same problem here.
Early-bye penalty — 20%
A week-5 bye is the dangerous one — your bench is as thin as it will ever be, almost nobody has been guillotined yet and there is nothing on waivers to cover the hole. By week 12 several whole rosters have hit FAAB and a bye is free. Tiebreaker between similar players, not a reason to reach.
Top-QB premium — 35%
QB is the steadiest weekly score on the board and every team starts one. This lifts only the quarterbacks worth reaching for and fades to nothing by the last starter, so it never inflates QB10–QB25 and never moves replacement level.
Guillotine is a weekly-lineup format that punishes one bad week harder than it rewards a great one, so floor, availability and a late bye move the board and a backup QB is dead weight. Best ball rewards spike weeks, so boom rate and ceiling move the board, QB2 is a real pick, and bench RB/WR keep value. Redraft sits between them and ignores both layers.
Seed with consensus rankings
Paste any ranking list — FantasyPros consensus, a guillotine cheat sheet, anywhere. Almost any format works. This replaces the built-in order and adds players the app doesn't know about. The format layer still runs on top of it: the pasted list is the consensus the floor, bye and QB weighting are measured against, exactly as the built-in one is.
